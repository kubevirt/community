# WG ARM architecture Charter

## Scope

The ARM architecture working group takes care of all things related to supporting ARM architecture on KubeVirt clusters.
This includes all build processes, also support of failing builds or test lanes.

## Communication

Contact the working group through the public KubeVirt community channels:

* [#kubevirt-dev on Kubernetes Slack](https://kubernetes.slack.com/archives/C0163DT0R8X)
* [kubevirt-dev mailing list](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/kubevirt-dev)

## Meetings

The working group does not currently have a recurring meeting scheduled.
Use the public channels above to discuss ARM topics or propose a meeting.
